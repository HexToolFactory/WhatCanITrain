# Changelog

## 1.0.0
- First public version for WoW: Forever.
- Training list in its own spellbook tab (class, weapon and pet training) and in the profession window.
- Lists are read from the trainer window on your first visit - no built-in spell data, so it always matches the game.
- Shopping list: every entry has a checkbox. Unchecked skills move to "Not Buying Now" (just above "Already Known") and are left out of every total, so "Coming Soon" and "Not Yet Available" only add up what you still want.
- Ctrl+click a checkbox or a row to check or uncheck every rank of that spell at once.
- "Buy All" button in the trainer window: learns every checked skill you can learn and afford right now (skips unchecked ones and new professions).
